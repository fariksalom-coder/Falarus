Registration video sequence assets used by UnpaidHomePaywall:

1. welcome-intro.mp4   — short opening video
2. welcome-tariffs.mp4 — course and tariff overview
3. welcome-bonus.mp4   — limited-time 3-month-price / 6-month-access offer

Every visit to the unpaid-course screen restarts the sequence and renews the bonus, including older registrations and previously expired offers. Production files are optimized H.264/AAC MP4s.

The countdown above the player follows the remaining media time: faster playback consumes it faster; pausing or buffering stops it.

Only after welcome-bonus.mp4 ends does the bonus purchase button replace the ordinary tariff cards and start a server-backed 10-minute purchase deadline. This purchase deadline is separate from the video countdown.
