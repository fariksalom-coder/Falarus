Registration video sequence assets used by UnpaidHomePaywall:

1. welcome-intro.mp4   — short opening video
2. welcome-tariffs.mp4 — course and tariff overview
3. welcome-bonus.mp4   — limited-time 3-month-price / 6-month-access offer

The registration sequence is available only when the user first opens the unpaid-course screen within 10 minutes of account creation. The purchase offer expires 10 minutes after the final video ends. Production files are optimized H.264/AAC MP4s.

At 25 seconds of welcome-bonus.mp4, the bonus purchase button replaces the ordinary tariff cards. Purchase is available immediately; the timer stays at 10:00 until the video ends, then counts down using the server deadline.
