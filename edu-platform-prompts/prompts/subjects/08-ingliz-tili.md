# Ingliz tili — kontent va vositalar

> Avval `_UMUMIY.md` ni o'qi. Fan rangi: `#DC2626`

## Tuzilma — CEFR darajalari bo'yicha
`A1 → A2 → B1 → B2 → C1`

Har daraja: Grammar · Vocabulary · Listening · Reading · Speaking · Writing

## Grammatika ketma-ketligi
**A1–A2**: to be, Present Simple, Present Continuous, Past Simple, articles, plurals, prepositions, can/must, comparatives, there is/are, Future (will/going to)
**B1**: Present Perfect, Past Continuous, conditionals 0–2, passive voice, modals, reported speech asoslari, gerund/infinitive
**B2**: Perfect Continuous zamonlar, conditionals 3 + mixed, advanced passive, relative clauses, inversion, phrasal verbs
**C1**: nuanced modality, cleft sentences, discourse markers, academic register

## Imtihonga tayyorgarlik modullari
- **DTM ingliz tili** — O'zbekiston formatida
- **IELTS** — 4 ta bo'lim, band descriptor'lar bilan baholash
- **CEFR milliy sertifikat (A2/B1/B2)** — O'zbekiston formatida

## Maxsus UI vositalari
- **Talaffuz mashqi**: o'quvchi gapiradi → speech-to-text → to'g'ri talaffuz bilan solishtirish, ball
- **Listening pleyeri**: tezlik boshqaruvi, transkript yoqish/o'chirish, qismini takrorlash
- **Lug'at (SRS)**: flashcard, spaced repetition, so'z + talaffuz + misol gap + rasm
- **Grammar trenajyori**: bo'sh joy to'ldirish, xatoni topish, gap qurish (drag & drop)
- **AI Speaking partner**: erkin suhbat, real vaqtda tuzatish, mavzu bo'yicha rol o'ynash
- **Writing checker**: essay/letter — grammatika, kogeziya, lug'at boyligi, struktura bo'yicha izoh
- **Reading**: matn ichida noma'lum so'z bosilsa tarjima (o'zbekcha), keyin lug'atga qo'shish

## Kontent tili
Dars materiali **inglizcha**, izoh va tushuntirish **o'zbekcha** (A1–B1 uchun).
B2+ dan boshlab tushuntirish ham inglizcha.
